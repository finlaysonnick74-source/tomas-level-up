How to support Tomas.

Thank you for being in Tomas's corner. By supporting him, you help him stay with Dad for the whole of this program, and finish something he can be really proud of.

💛 The one thing that matters most

For these 21 days, Tomas needs to be here at Dad's house, with Dad, and focused during the program sessions: 4:30–6:30pm on school days, and 2 hours at a time he chooses on weekends. That steady routine is what makes this work.

Everything else is warmly encouraged.

🏡 Visit any time

Mum, the grandparents, and Melanie and Sio (The Burdekin) are welcome to visit daily, or as often as you like. Come and see what he's building.

🎉 Treats and good times

Take him out for treats, movie nights, special dinners, have friends over, give financial rewards for milestones. All encouraged, as long as he's here for the sessions.

🎤 Show-and-tells

Fridays 23 Oct, 30 Oct and 6 Nov 2026 (Launch Day). Come in person or on video, and ask him a question or two.

📈 Check the tracker

The daily tracker shows each day's plan, what he built, what he learned and his streak. A quick message of encouragement goes a long way.
