How to support Tomas.

Thank you for being in Tomas's corner. Here are simple, positive ways to help over the 21 days.

🎤 Come to show-and-tell

Fridays 23 Oct, 30 Oct and 6 Nov 2026 (Launch Day), in person or on video. Tomas presents his week; ask him a question or two.

💬 Leave encouragement

A quick message after you check the tracker goes a long way. Notice the effort, not just the result.

📈 Check the tracker

The daily tracker shows each day's plan, what he built, what he learned and his streak.

👩 Mum

  • Comes to the Friday show-and-tells
  • Gets Tomas's weekly update every Friday
  • Joins the Sunday check-in
  • Backs him on school

👵👴 Grandparents

  • Cheer on the show-and-tells
  • Leave encouragement on his progress
  • Be his first "test customers": try the site and give feedback

🧭 School counsellor

  • Check the tracker and build log as evidence of routine
  • Talk with Tomas about the life skills he's practising
  • Help connect the skills back to school

👨 Dad

  • Works side by side every session
  • Plays the client and gives honest feedback
  • Looks after money and adult-only accounts

Great questions to ask Tomas

  • What are you proudest of this week?
  • Show me one thing you built and how it works.
  • What was hard, and how did you get past it?
  • What's your goal for next week?
