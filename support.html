What Tomas needs from all of us to make this a success.

Thank you for being in Tomas's corner. Here's how, together, we can help him finish something he'll be proud of for a long time.

1

This is Tomas's choice

Tomas really wants to do this. It's his business, his website and his future. He chose these 21 days, and the best thing any of us can do is help him see his choice through.

2

Why 21 days at Dad's, without interruption

Routine, discipline, structure and commitment aren't learned from talking about them. They're built by showing up at the same place, at the same time, every day. Research on habit formation backs this up: habits form through repeating the same action in the same setting, and consistency matters more than intensity (Lally et al., European Journal of Social Psychology, 2010).

So when he's pulled somewhere else, even with the very best intentions, the streak breaks and it gets harder for him to get going again. Keeping things steady is one of the kindest things we can do for him right now.

3

What we're asking of everyone

  • Back the plan out loud and give him one consistent message: "We're proud of you. Go for it."
  • Keep the 4:30–6:30pm sessions at Dad's protected on school days (and his 2 hours on weekends).
  • Please don't make plans that take him away from Dad's for the length of the program.
  • Check the tracker and celebrate his progress.
