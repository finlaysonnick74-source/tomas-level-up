📈 Forecast

A 24-month forecast (Nov 2026 to Oct 2028) in three scenarios, driven by sales. Every figure is a forecast, not a promise.

Charity website builds are done for free, so they are unpaid and not included in the revenue figures below. Giving back →

A forecast P&L for Nov 2026 to Oct 2028 in three versions. Each one is driven by sales: how many businesses we reach, how many chats that turns into, and how many say yes. Dad can build about 3 sites a week with Tomas and Grok Bot, so building is not the limit. Every figure here is a forecast, not a promise. Prices are the plan's own (section 2).

P&L: Profit and loss: money in, minus money out, month by month.

Revenue: All the money clients pay (build fees plus monthly fees), before costs.

Retainer: A fixed monthly fee for ongoing work: website care (A$99) or Instagram care (A$300 to A$500).

Lead: A business we contact: a walk-in, a DM, a referral or someone who messages us.

Conversion: The share of leads who get to a real sales chat, then the share of chats that buy (the win rate).

Gross / net profit: Gross: revenue minus card fees. Net: gross minus tools, Grok Bot share, Canva, setup and any helper.

Cumulative cash: Net profit added up month after month. Below zero means Dad has put in more than has come back.

Break-even: The first month cumulative cash is back to zero or above and stays there: Dad is paid back.

Churn: Monthly clients who stop paying. We assume 4–6% of them leave every month.

Assumptions

StartMonth 1 is Nov 2026. The first site (Mrs Jones The Baker) is built free as the showcase. In realistic and stretch, Mrs Jones pays for website care from Jan 2027. Dad funds every cost until the business pays it back.
Sales drive itLeads a week ramp up over the first 5 months (10%, 25%, 45%, 70%, then 100% of the full effort). A won client's site is built and paid the following month. From Oct 2027 leads step up to the year-2 level. Conversion rates are in section 11.
Build capacityAbout 3 sites a week, with Dad building alongside Tomas using Grok Bot. Tomas's 12-hours-a-week limit no longer sets the number of builds. Only stretch year 2 (about 4 a week) goes over it; each extra site costs a helper about A$700 (20 h at A$35/h, assumption).
PricesPlan prices only. Builds: A$1,500 (conservative, low end of the A$1,500–3,000 range) or A$1,800 (the plan's example). Website care A$99/mo. Instagram care A$300/mo, or A$350 in stretch (the plan's example average). No bundle discount.
Retainers + churnThe month after a build, a share of clients start care or Instagram (section 11). Each month 6% (conservative) or 4% (realistic, stretch) of monthly clients leave.
ToolsDad's budget tiers by monthly clients: Start lean A$392.68 (under 5), Growing A$503.91 (5–9), Pro A$556.93 (10–24). Scale tier (assumption) from 25 clients: Pro plus A$7.17 per Instagram client over 10 (Buffer channel) and an extra Netlify Pro (A$28.66) per 50 clients. Canva Pro A$13.75.
Grok BotTomas's share is A$347.50/mo (25% of Dad's real invoices, mid usage) at up to 4 builds a month, A$437.06 (budget high case) at 5–9, and A$695 (assumption: 50% share) at 10 or more.
Helper (assumption)Tomas can do about 45 hours of monthly client work (Instagram ~7 h, care ~1.5 h per client). Extra hours are paid to a helper at A$35/h. Check award wages and rules before hiring anyone.
Per clientClients register and pay for their own domains (A$9.95 first year, then A$22.95/yr), so A$0 here. Hosting is inside the A$99 care fee and Dad's Netlify plan. Card fees: Stripe 1.65% + A$0.30 per payment.
Not includedGST and income tax are not modelled. Prices are treated as the amount received. Not costed: Dad's and Tomas's own time, ads for the studio, travel and equipment. Sales time is an estimate (15 min per lead, 1 h per sales chat).
Check firstUnder-18 business matters (contracts, ABN, who owns the business, tax, GST once income passes A$75,000 a year, insurance in Dad's name, hiring a helper) should be checked with an accountant before the first paid job.
