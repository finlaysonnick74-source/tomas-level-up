💛 Giving back

I want to give back to my community and to the people who have supported me.

Why I’m doing it

Lots of people on the Northern Beaches have backed me. Building free websites for charities is my way of giving something back to the community and to the people who’ve supported me. It’s also something I’m genuinely proud to do.

🎁 What charities get

  • A free website build: modern, mobile-friendly, with a contact or donation link
  • Optional website care, discounted or free, so the site stays up to date
  • Help connecting their Instagram so it stays fresh

📅 How many

The plan: about 1 charity site per quarter, alongside my paid work. That way every build gets my full attention.

✉️ How a charity can ask

Get in touch through Tomas Web Studio, tell me about your charity and what you need, and we’ll have a chat with Dad about timing.

🌱 It helps me grow too

Every charity site adds to my portfolio and builds my skills, so giving back makes me better at what I do.

💛 A charity I’d love to build for: Burdekin

Burdekin is an independent not-for-profit with its head office in Brookvale. It works with young people and their families, helping prevent youth homelessness, providing safe housing and supporting wellbeing, and it leads the Beaches Youth Hub in Avalon. I’d love to offer Burdekin a free website build, if it would help them.

This is an offer and a hope, not an agreed project. Sources: burdekin.org.au, contact page, Beaches Youth Hub (viewed Oct 2026).
