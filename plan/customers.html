🌊 Customers and the Northern Beaches

Who I help first, and who their customers are.

A. Which businesses I go to first

TypeWhy they're good clients
Cafes and bakeriesLook great on Instagram; daily "fresh today" content; my showcase is a bakery
Salons (hair, nails, lashes)Before/after photos; bookings come from Instagram
Gyms and studiosClasses, timetables, transformations; need sign-ups
Surf shopsSummer rush, new stock, local surf culture
Trades (builders, plumbers, landscapers)Often have old or no websites; before/after jobs

Where, in order: 1 Freshwater 2 Manly 3 Dee Why 4 Mona Vale Start close to home, where the Mrs Jones example means most.

B. Who each client's customers are (for their Instagram)

Customer groupWhat they want to see
Locals aged 25–55 within ~10 kmWhat's fresh today, opening hours, specials, faces of the team (with permission)
ParentsKids' birthday cakes, school-holiday treats, easy weekend breakfasts
Event planners (for cakes and catering)Wedding and celebration cakes, catering for up to 500, how to book
Tourists in summerBeach-day coffee and gelato, "best bakery near Freshwater Beach"

C. Sample local ad setup (EXAMPLE ONLY)

SettingExample for Mrs Jones The Baker
GoalMore cake enquiries (people tap "Book a cake")
Location / radius~8–10 km around Freshwater
Age25–55
InterestsBakeries, coffee, brunch, weddings, birthday parties, parenting, Northern Beaches
AdShort reel of a cake being finished, with "Design your celebration cake"
BudgetAbout A$5–10 a day for 7 days (around A$35–70), paid by the client from their ad account
Check after 7 daysCost per click, enquiries, and whether to keep it, change it or stop

Ads run from the client's own Meta account. Meta needs adults to manage ad accounts and payments, so the owner (or Dad) controls the money side.
