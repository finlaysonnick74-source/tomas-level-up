🛡 Key risks

Every plan has risks. Here is each one, and what we do about it.

RiskWhat could happenWhat we do about it
Not enough leadsSales is the speed limit. If we reach half the leads, we win about half the sites. Walk-ins only happen after 4:30pm and on weekends.Track leads every week. Brookvale list first, then referrals and Instagram. Dad helps sell.
Lower win ratesOwners say “maybe later”. A 20% win rate instead of 30% cuts stretch builds by a third.Lead with the Mrs Jones showcase and a free check; follow up day 1 / 3 / 7.
Fixed costs earlyAbout A$393 a month with zero clients, mostly the Grok Bot share.First 2 paid sites cover it. Dad reviews the share each quarter.
Monthly work piles upEvery new client adds care or Instagram work. Realistic needs a helper from late year 1; stretch year 2 pays about A$148k for help.Postiz scheduling and Grok Bot templates to cut hours; hire help only when the money is in.
ChurnIf 8% leave a month instead of 4%, year-2 retainer income falls by about a fifth.Monthly report and check-in; show results.
Quality at speed3–4 sites a week can mean rushed sites and unhappy clients.Templates per trade; 2 rounds of changes; no promises we can't keep.
School, money and lawSchool comes first. Big numbers bring GST, tax, contracts and hiring rules.Accountant before the first paid job; Dad co-signs; 50% deposit on builds.

CONSERVATIVE

What has to be true

  • 12–15 leads a week, every week
  • 1 in 5 leads gets to a chat; 1 in 5 chats buys
  • About 2–3 sites a month at A$1,500
  • Dad can fund about A$1,300 for the first 3 months

REALISTIC

What has to be true

  • 16 leads a week by month 5, 24 in year 2
  • 1 in 4 chats buys, at A$1,800
  • About 1 site a week by month 6, 1.5 a week in year 2
  • Half of build clients keep website care

STRETCH

What has to be true

  • 2 sites a week by month 6, 4 a week in year 2
  • 44 leads a week in year 2: Dad and partners selling too
  • Extra build help for sites above 3 a week
  • A helper team for monthly client work
The bottom line (forecast): the money risk is small: the most Dad has in at any point is about A$1,296. The real question is sales: can we reach enough businesses every week? Count leads weekly. If after 3 months we are below the conservative line, change the plan.
