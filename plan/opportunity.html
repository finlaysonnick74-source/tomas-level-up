💡 The opportunity

Local businesses with a great story and an online shop window ready for a refresh.

Lots of cafes, bakeries, salons, gyms and tradies on the Beaches have an old website (or none) and an Instagram that's ready for fresh content. I use AI tools like Cursor ("vibe coding") to build good-looking websites fast, then keep their Instagram fresh every month.

The problem

Owners are busy and their online shop window is ready for a refresh.

My fix

A modern website in about 2 weeks, plus monthly Instagram posts.

Why it works

Fast to build means a fair price for them and good money for me.
