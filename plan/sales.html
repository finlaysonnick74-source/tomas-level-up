Following up

Most sales come from following up, not the first visit. Keep it friendly and short, and stop if they say no.

With new leads

WhenWhatWhat I say or do
Day 1Walk inQuiet time (not the lunch rush). Show the mock-up on my phone: "I made this for a bakery in Freshwater. Want me to do a free check of your website and Instagram?" Leave a card.
Day 3Message (Instagram DM or email)"Thanks for chatting on Tuesday. Here's your free one-page check with 3 quick wins." Attach it.
Day 7Phone call"Did the 3 tips make sense? Would you like a quote?" If not now, ask if I can check back in 2 months.

I keep every lead in a simple sheet: name, business, date, next step, result.

Sales is the speed limit

The big idea: we can build about 3 sites a week. What we can't do yet is find and win 3 clients a week. Growth is set by sales and marketing, not by building. Every extra site needs about 11 to 25 businesses reached first.

CONSERVATIVE

What sales must happen

  • Mrs Jones showcase finished and approved to show
  • Brookvale walk-ins: the 17 prospects first (score 4–5), 2–3 visits on school days
  • Day 1 / 3 / 7 follow-ups on every lead
  • Tomas's own Instagram posting 3 times a week, scheduled in Postiz
  • About 12 leads a week, rising to 15

REALISTIC

What sales must happen

  • All of conservative, plus Manly, Dee Why and Freshwater walk-ins
  • A free website + Instagram check for every warm lead
  • Ask every client for a review and 2 referral names
  • Before/after posts of each new site via Postiz; Google Business Profile
  • About 16 leads a week by month 5, 24 in year 2

STRETCH

What sales must happen

  • All of realistic, plus Dad selling to his network and running sales chats
  • Local ads for the studio (Meta, small budget, not costed)
  • Partners: accountants, sign-writers, printers who refer clients
  • A case study page per trade (bakery, mechanic, groomer)
  • About 22 leads a week by month 5, 44 in year 2 (a part-time job)
