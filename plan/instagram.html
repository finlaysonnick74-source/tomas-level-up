📸 Instagram strategy

Real, local and simple beats fancy. Film once, post all month.

Every client gets a mix of 4 post types. Real, local and simple beats fancy.

Post typeWhat it isBakery example
Behind the scenesThe work: prep, tools, the process. Hands are fine, no faces without permission.Loaves going into the four-deck oven
Fresh todayWhat's new, what's ready now, what's nearly gone."Seeded rye just out, 6 left"
StoryWho they are and why they care.The Savoy to Freshwater; why bread takes two days
Customer postsReposts of customer photos and reviews, always with their OK.A customer's birthday cake photo

One post of each type a week (4 posts). On the basic plan, skip one a week: about 12 posts a month, plus stories on most days.

The monthly batch: film once, post all month

1 · Plan

30 min with the owner: what's coming up (specials, events, holidays)? Write a shot list.

2 · Film

One 1–2 hour visit, ideally on a weekend morning. Phone, good light, short clips and photos.

3 · Edit

Canva/CapCut: 12 posts, captions, hashtags (#Freshwater #NorthernBeaches). Owner approves.

4 · Schedule

Load them into Meta Business Suite (free). Add live "fresh today" stories through the month.
