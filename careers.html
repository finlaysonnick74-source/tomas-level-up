Routes for a 15-year-old

How to get there from here

📘 School subjects (NSW HSC)

Software Engineering (programming, web, secure software, automation) and Enterprise Computing (UX, networking, cybersecurity, data). Both started in 2024, replacing older courses. NSW Education

🏫 TAFE NSW short courses

Short courses in web, coding, cybersecurity and digital marketing: a great way to test interests while still at school. tafensw.edu.au

🛠 School-based traineeship

Start a nationally recognised IT or digital traineeship while still doing the HSC: paid work and a qualification together. NSW SBATs

🏅 Certifications

AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, Google Career Certificates (e.g. digital marketing, data analytics, UX). Online, self-paced, respected by employers.

🧑‍💻 Work experience + hackathons

Year 10–11 work experience at a tech company or agency, plus hackathons and coding competitions. Real projects, real mentors, a great portfolio.

🏛 Government programs

APS Data and Digital Cadet Program: for uni students in data or digital degrees; part-time work while studying, then a full-time role (APSJobs). The older APS Digital Traineeship has closed. ASD Cadetship: for university STEM students, paid study plus work at ASD (ASD careers). Defence also has its own cyber and ICT careers.
