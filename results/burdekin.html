What I could do for The Burdekin

Some ideas for how I could help The Burdekin’s online presence, to show what I’m learning.

⚠️ This is a concept to show my skills. The Burdekin hasn’t asked for or approved this. It uses no Burdekin logo or images.

Why The Burdekin

From their website, The Burdekin is an independent not-for-profit that has worked with young people and their families for nearly 40 years, helping to prevent youth homelessness, provide safe housing and support wellbeing. Their programs include youth housing, family support, out-of-home care and community outreach, and people can help by volunteering, fostering, fundraising or donating. Melanie and Sio are part of my crew, and I’d love to give something back.

My ideas

🌱 A living website

  • A fresh “news and stories” strip that’s easy for staff to update weekly
  • Upcoming events and fundraisers on a simple calendar
  • Impact numbers that update as the year goes on

📱 Social integration

  • Latest Instagram posts shown on the home page
  • Posts planned a week ahead with Postiz, so it’s never a rush
  • Share buttons on stories and fundraisers

♿ Accessibility

  • Strong colour contrast (WCAG AA) and large, readable text
  • Everything works with a keyboard and a screen reader
  • Plain-English labels and alt text on every image
  • Fast on any phone, even on slow data

🤝 Clear ways to help

  • A simple “How can you help?” chooser: Donate · Volunteer · Foster · Fundraise
  • A clear donate button on every page
  • A short volunteer interest form

🧭 Help, fast

  • A calm, easy-to-find “Need help?” path for young people and families, with the phone number one tap away on mobile
