In my words

Why staying in school is part of my plan.

I want to build a real business and a great career. Finishing school while I do this program is how I open the most doors.

Every path deserves respect

Trades, apprenticeships, starting work, uni: people I respect have taken all of them, and they're all great paths. I'm choosing this one because it fits what I love: tech, building things and running my own business.

📘 School subjects that fit my plan

The NSW HSC now has two courses made for what I'm doing: Software Engineering (programming, web, secure software, automation) and Enterprise Computing (UX, networking, cybersecurity, data). They started in 2024. (NSW Education)

🎓 TAFE and uni pathways

Finishing school keeps every option open: TAFE NSW courses in IT and digital, uni degrees in software, cyber or data, and programs like the APS Data and Digital Cadet Program and the ASD Cadetship, which are for uni students. (APSJobs, ASD)

🏅 Certifications alongside school

I can add things like AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and Google Career Certificates while I study.

💼 Where it leads

IT careers pay well. Jobs and Skills Australia reports median full-time weekly earnings of A$2,537 for software and applications programmers and A$2,476 for web developers and multimedia specialists, compared with A$1,852 across all occupations (ABS, May 2025). See all the careers →

Sources: JSA 2613, JSA 2612, viewed Oct 2026.

Why this one is mine

School gives me the qualifications. This program is my chance to gain real skills, a real business and a routine I'm proud of. Together they open the most doors, and I want all of them open.
