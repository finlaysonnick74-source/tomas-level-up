Web Basics · Lesson 3 of 11 · Domains & hosting

The address and
the house.

Every website needs two things: a name people can type, and a place where its files live. They're often bought from different companies and then connected together.

Domain · the addressRented yearly from a registrar
DNS · the signpostSet in DNS settings
Hosting · the houseProvided by a host, e.g. Netlify

Domains

You don't buy a domain forever — you rent it, usually one or more years at a time, from a registrar (a company allowed to sell domain names). Australian registrars include VentraIP and Crazy Domains; there are many others.

A .com.au name needs an Australian business connection, such as an ABN (Australian Business Number — the ID number for a business). Dad can help you sort that out.

Forget to renew, and someone else can grab the name. Always turn on auto-renew.

Hosting

Most small business websites are static sites: the same files are sent to every visitor. These are fast, cheap (often free) and very hard to hack. Netlify is great at these.

Good hosts give you HTTPS for free — the padlock in the address bar, meaning the connection is private and secure. Every site you make should have it.

Pro tip for client work

Set up each client's domain in their name and account, with you helping. It's their business — and it makes hand-over clean and trustworthy.
