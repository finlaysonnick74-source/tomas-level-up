🧰 Squoosh

What it is

A free Google web app that makes image files smaller without looking worse.

Why you’ll use it

Small images mean fast pages, and fast pages keep visitors happy on their phones.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Open squoosh.app.
  2. Drop in a photo.
  3. Choose WebP and move the quality slider.
  4. Compare the before and after sizes.
  5. Download the smaller version.

👨 Account note

Free, with no account needed.

🚀 Mini challenge

Shrink a photo to under 200 KB without it looking blurry.

✅ Ready when

You can show Dad Squoosh working at the toolkit check at the end of Day 3.
