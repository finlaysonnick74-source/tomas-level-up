🧰 Netlify (hosting)

What it is

A host that puts your site online and updates it every time you push to GitHub.

Why you’ll use it

It’s how your business site goes live: test addresses, preview links, the contact form and, on Launch Day, your own domain.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Sign up to Netlify with your GitHub account.
  2. Connect your tomas-web-studio repo.
  3. Deploy it and open your free test address.
  4. Change a line in your README or page, push it, and watch Netlify update by itself.
  5. Bookmark your site’s Netlify dashboard.
