📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Analytics: tools that count visits: how many people came, from where, and what they looked at.

What Search Console does and what analytics count. Verify your site and send Google your sitemap (a list of your pages).

  • Google Search Console: A free Google tool that shows whether Google has found your pages and how people search for you.
  • Google Analytics: A free tool that counts visits: how many, from where, and which pages.
  • Grok Bot: An AI assistant that writes and changes code when you describe what you want.
  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.
