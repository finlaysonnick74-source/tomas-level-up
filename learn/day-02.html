📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Deploy: to put the latest version of your site onto the internet so everyone can see it.

Web Basics lessons 7–11: deployment, old vs modern, your toolkit map, the project roadmap and the jargon buster.

  • Grok Bot: An AI assistant that writes and changes code when you describe what you want.
  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.
  • Netlify: A host that puts your site online and updates it every time you push to GitHub.
  • Canva: An easy design app with templates for logos, images and social posts.
  • Postiz: A scheduler: plan a batch of posts and it publishes them on time for you.

💬 Talk it through with Dad

To finish the session, sit down together for a relaxed chat about today’s lessons. There are no wrong answers, it’s just a chance to put the ideas into your own words.

  1. What does it mean to deploy a site, and how does it happen automatically with Netlify?
  2. What makes a modern website better than an old one? Name three things.
  3. Pick three tools from your toolkit map. What job does each one do?
  4. Walk through the project roadmap: brief, design, build, test, deploy, hand-over. Which step are you most excited about?
  5. How do coding, hosting, deployment and change control all fit together on your own site?
  6. Which words from the jargon buster would you use to explain your business to a client?

🛠️ Dive in (optional)

Keen to get your hands dirty? Whenever you feel like it, open your business site and start tinkering: change a heading, try a colour, ask Grok Bot to add a section. Totally optional, and the brief and real building start on Day 3.

❓ Quick quiz

1. What does deploy mean?

Putting the latest version of your site on the internet.

2. Name one thing that makes a modern site better than an old one.

For example: works on phones, loads fast, clear call to action, easy to update.

3. What is a brief?

A short written plan of what a site must do, for whom, by when and for how much.
