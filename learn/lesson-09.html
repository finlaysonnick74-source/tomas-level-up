Working well with Grok Bot

  • Be specific: “Make the Book Now button bigger and orange on phones”.
  • Ask why it did something. That's how you learn fast.
  • Always check the preview yourself before it goes live.

Golden rules

  • Never paste passwords or a client's private info into a chat.
  • AI can make mistakes. You're the one responsible for what ships.
  • Only use images and fonts you're allowed to use.
