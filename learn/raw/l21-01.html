Hey Tomas —
21 days. Let’s go.

This is a turnaround, and you’re the one turning it. For 21 days you’ll spend 2 hours a day with Dad building your real business website. Along the way you’ll build things that matter even more: routine, follow-through, and work you’re proud of.

Your school-day rhythm

Weekends: same 2 hours, you pick when (for example 10am–12pm). Gym with Mum is the one exception to 4:30pm: on those days the session starts once you’re home and homework is done.

Every session, the same 5 steps

Your job

  • Turn up on time, every day, weekends too.
  • Do the work, ask “why?”, finish what you start.
  • Commit your work and write one journal line.

Dad’s job

  • Sits beside you and keeps you on track.
  • Plays the client, reviews your work, gives honest feedback.
  • Handles money and adult-only accounts.

Mum’s part

  • Comes to each Friday show-and-tell.
  • Gets your weekly update every Friday.
  • Backs you on school, and at Sunday check-ins.

Ground rules for the 21 days

1. School comes first. At school on time, homework done before you build.
2. Every day counts. 21 sessions, weekends included.
3. Commit every session. Your saved work is your proof.
4. Own it. No excuses, no blaming. Fix it and move on.
5. Stay safe online. No passwords or private info in chats or posts.
6. Permission first. Nothing about anyone goes public without a yes.

How to use this plan: each day has its own card (from page 8). Tick your daily log (page 26) and trackers (pages 27–28) yourself. Nobody signs anything: this works because you chose it.
