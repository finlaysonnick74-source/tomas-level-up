For Melanie, Sio and Mum

How progress is evidenced.

For Melanie and Sio (The Burdekin) and for Mum: six kinds of evidence, most of it dated automatically, so progress can be seen rather than taken on faith.

1

GitHub commit history

Every session ends with a dated save (a commit) and a message saying what was done. At least 21 by Day 21. Ask Tomas to open the project’s Commits page: it’s a timestamped diary that can’t be back-filled neatly.

2

The live site

Online at a test address from Day 7 and on his own domain from Day 16. Netlify keeps a dated list of every update. Today’s early showcase: finlaysonnick74-source.github.io/tomas-web-studio.

3

The daily log

Tomas ticks it himself each night (page 26): up on time, at school on time, home by 4:30pm, session done, commit pushed, journal line.

4

School attendance kept

On-time attendance is part of the log every school day, so it can be compared with the school’s own records. School always comes before the build.

5

Weekly show-and-tell

Fri 23 Oct, 30 Oct and 6 Nov. Tomas presents to Mum and the family, then sends Mum a short weekly update with links.

6

Skills tracker & reflection

21 technical skills, 14 tools and 11 life skills ticked and scored by Tomas, plus a journal line every day and a self-review every week.

What good looks like on Day 21

  • 21 sessions logged, weekends included
  • At least 21 dated commits on GitHub
  • School attendance ticked every school day
  • Site live on his own domain by Fri 6 Nov
  • 3 show-and-tells held and 3 weekly updates sent to Mum
  • 3 weekly self-reviews and 21 journal lines

How to check in

  • Ask Tomas to show you his commits from the last few days.
  • Open the live site on your phone and send an enquiry.
  • Ask: “What are you proud of this week?”

If a day goes wrong

It’s logged honestly, not hidden. The next day starts fresh at the usual time. One missed day doesn’t undo the other twenty: owning it and showing up again is the skill.
