The framework

Life skills this builds.

The website is the vehicle. These eleven life skills are the destination. Each one is practised on specific days and leaves evidence anyone can check. Every day card is tagged with its life skills.

Life skillWhat it looks likeDaysEvidence
Routine
Commitment, discipline & routine		Home by 4:30pm, up on time, session starts on time, every day for 21 days.Every day (focus: 5–7, 14)Daily log: up on time, home by 4:30pm, on-time start. A dated commit every session.
School first
School attendance on time		At school on time every school day.Every dayDaily log: at school on time. Can be compared with school records.
Planning
Planning, time management & deadlines		Plans the session, works to the timetable, hits the Day 21 launch date.1–3, 6, 15, 17, 19–20Timetable kept; weekly milestones met on their dates; launch on Day 21.
Goals
Goal-setting		Sets a goal each week and a bigger one for the next 21 days.1, 21Weekly goals in each self-review; a next-21-day goal on Day 21.
Money
Money sense		Budget, pricing, costs and profit (P&L), using his real business plan.1, 8, 16, 21Services & prices page; domain cost in the budget; price and profit check.
Communication
Communication		Explains his work, writes to a “client”, phone manner, presents at show-and-tell.2, 5, 7, 9–10, 12–14, 18, 20–21Three show-and-tells; client message (Day 12); phone pitch (Day 18); captions.
Ownership
Responsibility & ownership		Owns his work and his mistakes. No excuses, no blaming.4, 8–9, 11, 15, 20Bugs logged and closed in GitHub; honest self-reviews.
Resilience
Resilience		Sticks with bugs and setbacks until they’re fixed.5, 11–12, 15Fixed bugs; before-and-after Lighthouse scores; nothing left half-done.
Teamwork
Teamwork with Dad		Listens, takes feedback, and works side by side.3–4, 8, 12, 16, 19Dad’s reviews and approvals, visible on every pull request.
Reflection
Self-reflection		A journal line every day and an honest self-review every week.Every day (journal); focus: 7, 14, 17, 2121 journal lines, 3 weekly self-reviews, a Day 21 retro.
Online safety
Online safety & professional conduct		Protects privacy, asks permission, behaves like a professional online.9–10, 13, 16, 18Privacy and permission checks; nothing private on the site or Instagram.
