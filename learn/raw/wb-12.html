Your next three moves.

We'll learn by doing. Your very first client is you: Tomas's own business website. Each step walks through the same stages from the roadmap.

Stages we'll cover: 1 Brief2 Design3 Build4 Test5 Deploy6 Hand-over7 Updates

Things to try this week

  • Open a local business's website, right-click and choose View page source. Can you spot <h1> and <p> tags?
  • Right-click a heading and choose Inspect. Change its colour (only you can see it — it's harmless).
  • Create a free GitHub account and your first repository.
  • Ask Grok Bot for a one-page “Hello Northern Beaches” site. Read the code and find the HTML, CSS and JS.
  • Make a commit with a good message, then try a branch and a pull request.
  • Connect that repo to Netlify and open your first preview link on your phone.
  • Save five designs you love on Mobbin and write one line on why each works.
  • Make a logo idea and three Instagram posts in Canva; schedule one with Postiz.
  • List your services and a first guess at prices, ready to talk through with Dad.
  • Teach Dad one thing from this guide in your own words.
