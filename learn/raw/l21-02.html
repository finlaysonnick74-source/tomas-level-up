For Mum and Melanie and Sio from The Burdekin

A structured transformation,
built on real work.

This page is for Mum and for Melanie and Sio from The Burdekin. It explains what the next 21 days are, how they’re structured, and how everyone can see that Tomas is sticking with it.

What this is

A 21-day program, Sat 17 Oct to Fri 6 Nov 2026, while Tomas lives with Dad. It’s the “Build” part of the first phase (Reset) of his Level up 12-month game plan. The business website is the vehicle. The destination is character and life skills: routine, responsibility, resilience, communication and pride in his work (page 4).

How it’s structured

  • Same time, every day. Weekdays 4:30–6:30pm, straight after school at Dad’s. Weekends: 2 hours, flexible.
  • Same routine. Check-in, learn and build, short break, build, then reflect, save (commit) and a journal line.
  • One clear output every day, and a milestone at the end of every week.
  • Weekly show-and-tell for Mum and the family, then a self-review at the Sunday check-in.

Why real work

Teenagers build self-respect by making something real that other people can see and use. Every day ends with a finished piece of work, so progress is visible, not promised. The skills carry straight back into school: planning, deadlines, explaining his thinking, and sticking at hard problems.

School comes first

Sessions start at 4:30pm, straight after school, at Dad’s. Being at school on time is ticked every day in his log, alongside the build.

Nothing to sign

There are no signatures or sign-offs here. This rests on trust and on Tomas choosing it. The evidence (page 29) speaks for itself.

Who does what

Tomas

Shows up on time, does the work, keeps his own log and trackers, writes his journal line, and presents his week at show-and-tell.

Dad (Nick)

Works side by side every session, keeps him on track, reviews his work, and looks after money, contracts and adult-only accounts.

Mum

Comes to the Friday show-and-tells (in person or video), gets Tomas’s weekly update, joins the Sunday check-in, and encourages school attendance.
