10 · The stages of a project

Every website follows
the same road.

Whether it's your own site or a client's, a project moves through seven stages. Skipping one usually means trouble later.

Brief — a short written description of what the client wants: goals, pages, style, deadline and budget. It's your shared “plan”.

Hand-over — the moment you formally give the finished site to the client, along with everything they need to own and run it.

Ongoing updates (sometimes called a care plan or retainer) — a regular paid arrangement where you keep the site and Instagram fresh each month.

Great questions for any brief

  • What should a visitor do on your site? Call, book, buy, visit?
  • Who are your customers, and where do they find you?
  • Which websites do you love, and why?
  • Do you have a logo, photos and a domain already?
  • When do you need it, and what's your budget?

Always agree the price and what's included before you start building.
