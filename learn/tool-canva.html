🧰 Canva

What it is

An easy design app with templates for logos, images and social posts.

Why you’ll use it

You’ll make your logo, brand kit, website images and Instagram posts here.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Join Dad’s Canva team (Canva Pro), or set up your own account with Dad.
  2. Explore the templates for logos and Instagram posts.
  3. Create a folder called Tomas Web Studio.
  4. Try a simple design: your studio name in two different fonts.
  5. Download it as a PNG.

👨 Account note

Canva Pro is on Dad’s account, so Dad adds you to his team.

🚀 Mini challenge

Make 3 quick versions of your studio name in different colours. Which one feels most like you?

✅ Ready when

You can show Dad Canva working at the toolkit check at the end of Day 3.
