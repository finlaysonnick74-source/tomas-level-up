📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Rollback: switching the live site back to an earlier version that worked, in one click.

How to undo safely: revert a commit in GitHub, and roll back a deploy in Netlify.

  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.
  • Netlify: A host that puts your site online and updates it every time you push to GitHub.
  • Grok Bot: An AI assistant that writes and changes code when you describe what you want.

❓ Quick quiz

1. What does 'roll back' mean?

Putting the live site back to an earlier working version.

2. Why review before merging?

A second pair of eyes catches mistakes before the public sees them.

3. How do you stay calm when a client asks for changes?

Listen, write it down, agree what and when, then do it on a branch.
