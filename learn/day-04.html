📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Inspect: a browser tool that shows the code behind any part of a page. Changes only happen on your screen.

What a brief is and why every project starts with one. Dad interviews you with the 5 brief questions. Then: Browse Mobbin for great landing pages, pricing pages and contact forms. Save 10 screens to a collection.

  • Grok Bot: An AI assistant that writes and changes code when you describe what you want.
  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.
  • Mobbin: A huge library of screenshots from real, well-designed apps and websites.
  • Browser DevTools: Tools built into Chrome (right-click → Inspect) that show the code behind any page.

❓ Quick quiz

1. What 4 things does a brief cover?

Who the site is for, what it must do, by when, and for how much.

2. What is a commit?

A saved snapshot of your project with a short message saying what changed.

3. Does changing a page in Inspect change the real website?

No. It only changes it on your own screen until you refresh.

4. What's a 'steal list'?

Your shortlist of good ideas from other designs to adapt for your own site.
