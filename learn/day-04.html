📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Wireframe: a simple black-and-white sketch of a page showing where things go, with no colours or photos yet.

Browse Mobbin for great landing pages, pricing pages and contact forms. Save 10 screens to a collection. Then: What a site map and a wireframe are. “Mobile first”: design for the phone, then the laptop.

  • Mobbin: A huge library of screenshots from real, well-designed apps and websites.
  • Browser DevTools: Tools built into Chrome (right-click → Inspect) that show the code behind any page.
  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.
  • Figma: A design tool pros use to lay out pages before they are built.

❓ Quick quiz

1. Does changing a page in Inspect change the real website?

No. It only changes it on your own screen until you refresh.

2. What's a 'steal list'?

Your shortlist of good ideas from other designs to adapt for your own site.

3. What is a wireframe?

A plain sketch of where things go on a page, with no colours or photos.

4. What does 'mobile first' mean?

Design for the phone screen first, then expand for bigger screens.
