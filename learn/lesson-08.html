The old way vs
the modern way.

Websites have been around since the early 1990s. Knowing how it used to be done helps you understand why today's tools are so good — and you'll still bump into old setups at clients' businesses.

Old words you'll hear

FTP (File Transfer Protocol) — an old way of copying files from your computer straight onto a server, using an app like FileZilla.

cPanel — a control panel many traditional hosting companies give you for managing a server: files, email accounts, databases and so on.

Hand-written HTML — typing every tag yourself in a text editor. Still a great way to learn, but slow for real projects.

Business tip: a client stuck on an old cPanel or WordPress site that's slow and hard to update is a great lead. You can offer to move them to a faster, safer modern setup.
