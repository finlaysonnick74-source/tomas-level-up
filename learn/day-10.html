📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Form: the boxes on a page where visitors type their details and press Send.

How forms work and how Netlify Forms collects messages for you. Only ask for what you need: that’s privacy.

  • Grok Bot: An AI assistant that writes and changes code when you describe what you want.
  • Netlify: A host that puts your site online and updates it every time you push to GitHub.
  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.

❓ Quick quiz

1. Why no home address or personal phone on the site?

To keep you safe; use a business email and form instead.

2. Where does a form submission go?

To the host's form service (e.g. Netlify Forms), which emails it to you.

3. What should an About page do?

Build trust: who you are, why you do it, and why locals can rely on you.
