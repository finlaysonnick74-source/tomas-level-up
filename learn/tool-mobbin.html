🧰 Mobbin

What it is

A huge library of screenshots from real, well-designed apps and websites.

Why you’ll use it

Great designers collect ideas first. Mobbin shows you what really good pages look like.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Sign up with Dad for a free account.
  2. Search for “landing page” and “pricing page”.
  3. Make a collection called Tomas Web Studio ideas.
  4. Save 3 screens you love.
  5. Write one line about why each one works.

👨 Account note

Free plan. If it asks for a card for a paid tier, skip it, as the free one is enough.

🚀 Mini challenge

Find one design you don’t like and explain what you’d change.

✅ Ready when

You can show Dad Mobbin working at the toolkit check at the end of Day 3.
