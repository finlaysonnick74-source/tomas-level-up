Learn

A real 21-day program.

2 hours a day, side by side with Dad, Sat 17 Oct to Fri 6 Nov 2026. Each day has a timetable, objectives, the lesson, build tasks on the business site, a challenge, a quiz and a reflection.

🎯 By Day 21, I can… (the outcomes)

Week 1

Plan & design

This week you think like a designer. You’ll work out what your site must do, collect ideas, design your brand, write your first code with Grok Bot and put it on the internet.

  • Day 1 · Sat 17 Oct: Kick-off: write your brief
  • Day 2 · Sun 18 Oct: Research: steal like an artist
  • Day 3 · Mon 19 Oct: Site map & wireframes
  • Day 4 · Tue 20 Oct: Your brand kit in Canva
  • Day 5 · Wed 21 Oct: First code with Grok Bot
  • Day 6 · Thu 22 Oct: Homepage v1: hero, services, action
  • Day 7 · Fri 23 Oct: Online with Netlify + show-and-tell 🎤

Milestone: Brief, research, wireframes, brand kit, and a homepage online at a Netlify test address.

Week overview · Self-review

Week 2

Build it properly

This week you build like a pro. Every change happens on a branch, gets checked on a preview link, then joins the main site. You’ll test, fix, and set up your Instagram.

  • Day 8 · Sat 24 Oct: Services & prices (on a branch)
  • Day 9 · Sun 25 Oct: “Our work”: your showcase
  • Day 10 · Mon 26 Oct: About & a working contact form
  • Day 11 · Tue 27 Oct: Test like a pro
  • Day 12 · Wed 28 Oct: Change control: undo, review, roll back
  • Day 13 · Thu 29 Oct: Your Instagram business account
  • Day 14 · Fri 30 Oct: Polish for Google + show-and-tell 🎤

Milestone: The full 5-page site with services and prices, a showcase, About and a working contact form, built on branches, tested, plus an Instagram business account ready.

Week overview · Self-review

Week 3

Launch & get seen

This week your site goes live and starts working for you. You’ll check everything, connect your domain, get onto Google, join up Instagram and schedule your launch.

  • Day 15 · Sat 31 Oct: The launch checklist
  • Day 16 · Sun 1 Nov: Domain day: go live!
  • Day 17 · Mon 2 Nov: Get found on Google
  • Day 18 · Tue 3 Nov: Google Business Profile
  • Day 19 · Wed 4 Nov: Instagram ↔ website link-up
  • Day 20 · Thu 5 Nov: Schedule a week with Postiz
  • Day 21 · Fri 6 Nov: Launch day + show-and-tell 🎤

Milestone: Live on your own domain with HTTPS, found on Google, analytics running, Instagram linked, launch posts scheduled in Postiz: launched!
