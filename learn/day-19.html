📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Tracking link: a link with a tag on the end (like ?utm_source=instagram) so analytics knows where visitors came from.

Linking both ways: your site in your bio, Instagram buttons on your site, and tracking links.

  • Instagram (business): A business account on Instagram, with Insights: numbers on who saw and tapped your posts.
  • Grok Bot: An AI assistant that writes and changes code when you describe what you want.
  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.
  • Netlify: A host that puts your site online and updates it every time you push to GitHub.
  • Google Analytics: A free tool that counts visits: how many, from where, and which pages.

❓ Quick quiz

1. What is a 'living website'?

A site that updates itself from Instagram, reviews and other feeds.

2. How do you know visitors came from Instagram?

Analytics shows the source of each visit.

3. Why link both ways?

Followers find your site and site visitors find your Instagram.
