📚 The lesson

Word of the day: Retro: short for retrospective: looking back on a project to see what to keep doing and what to change.

Read your first numbers: Instagram Insights, analytics, your GitHub commits. Make a “21 days in numbers” card in Canva.

  • Postiz: A scheduler: plan a batch of posts and it publishes them on time for you.
  • Instagram (business): A business account on Instagram, with Insights: numbers on who saw and tapped your posts.
  • Google Analytics: A free tool that counts visits: how many, from where, and which pages.
  • GitHub: A website that keeps your code safe online, with every version saved.
  • Canva: An easy design app with templates for logos, images and social posts.
