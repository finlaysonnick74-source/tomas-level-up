9Let's talk

Next comes a family meeting with Mum and Dad, together. You get your say, and if you want to, you say what you're choosing, in your own words. Then a check-in with Dad and Mum every Sunday.

Questions to talk through

  1. Picture yourself in 12 months. What do you most want to be true?
  2. Which 2–3 business ideas made you think "that could be cool"? Why?
  3. Is there something you're into that isn't in here?
  4. Which one would you still enjoy after six months?
  5. Which founder story stuck with you, and why?
  6. What would you most like to build with Cursor: a shop, a booking site, an app, a bot?
  7. How many hours a week feels realistic with school and sport?
  8. What would help you keep a clear head, and what gets in the way? (No judgement, just honest.)
  9. Which subject could help your business most? What would make getting to school on time easier?
  10. Which sport or club would you try first, and who could come with you?
  11. Who in your life would back you if you started something? Who would you like to know?
  12. Which rewards on the scorecard ladder feel fair to you? What would you add?
  13. What would "success" look like for you by the end of summer?
  14. What would you want Mum and Dad to help with, and what do you want to do yourself?
