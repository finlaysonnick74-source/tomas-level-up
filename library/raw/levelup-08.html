PILLAR 3

5Train: sport & teams near you

Real clubs on the Northern Beaches that take 15-year-olds. Pick one or two to try. A team gives you training times to keep, coaches who back you, and new mates who are going somewhere.

Surf life saving
Manly LSC · manlylsc.com · Dee Why SLSC · dyslsc.org.au
What it is
Patrol the beach, train in the surf, compete in board and swim races.
What it builds
Fitness, calm under pressure, a real qualification at 15.
How to join
Do the Bronze Medallion. You need to be 15 on assessment day and swim 400m in under 9 minutes. Clubs run courses, and there's a Northern Beaches 4-day intensive listed for 14–17 Dec 2026 (surflifesaving.net.au).
Boxing at PCYC Northern Beaches
40 Kingsway, Dee Why · pcycnsw.org.au/northern-beaches
What it is
Non-contact teen boxing (ages 12–17), Mondays 5:30–6:15pm.
What it builds
Discipline, focus, fitness, self-control.
How to join
Book online. Listed at $125 a term or $15 casual, no experience needed. They also run basketball and soccer, plus "Fit for Life", a morning fitness and mentoring program that gets you to school.
Rugby league
Manly Junior District Rugby League · manlyjuniors.com.au
What it is
Junior teams at local clubs across the Beaches, in the Sea Eagles' pathway.
What it builds
Toughness, mateship, backing your teammates.
How to join
Pick a local club and register through playrugbyleague.com.
Rugby union
Warringah Junior Rugby Union · warringahjuniorrugby.com.au
What it is
Village clubs (Collaroy, Dee Why, Narrabeen, Newport, Pittwater, Wakehurst and more) feeding the Warringah Rats.
What it builds
Teamwork, structure, a big local network.
How to join
Register with your nearest village club.
Basketball
Manly Warringah Basketball · manlybasketball.com.au
What it is
Local comps, plus the Manly Sea Eagles junior rep teams (U12–U18).
What it builds
Quick decisions, fitness, being coachable.
How to join
Sign up for a domestic comp team, or ask about rep trials.
Football (soccer)
Manly Warringah Football Association · mwfa.com.au
What it is
The local association behind dozens of clubs. Winter season, plus summer comps.
What it builds
Fitness, teamwork, a weekly routine.
How to join
Pick a club and register on PlayFootball (juniors run U6–U18).
