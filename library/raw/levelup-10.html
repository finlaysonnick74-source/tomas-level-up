PILLAR 4

6Crew

This page is about something positive. It's about one simple, honest idea: the people you spend the most time with shape what feels normal to you.

Why it matters

If your crew trains, you train. If your crew stays up till 2am, so do you. If your crew is building things, you start building things. It works in both directions, and it happens to everyone, adults included. Founders choose their circle on purpose, because it's the fastest way to level up.

What it's not

  • Dumping your mates
  • Thinking you're better than anyone
  • Dad picking your friends

What it is

  • Adding new people who are heading where you want to go
  • Spending more time in places that build you up
  • Noticing how you feel after hanging out

Three easy ways to build a new crew

1. Teams

Training twice a week means seeing the same people who also show up. That's how most good friendships start.

2. Business

Customers, suppliers, other young founders, people online who build stuff. Dad's network too.

3. Work

A part-time job, a surf club patrol or helping at a local shop. You get paid and meet people a few steps ahead of you.

A quick self-check (just for you, no one else)

  1. Who do I feel more like myself around?
  2. Who would back me if I started a business tomorrow?
  3. Is there anything I keep doing mainly because the group does it?
Real friends want you to win. A clean record, a team and a business all open doors. The right crew helps you walk through them.
