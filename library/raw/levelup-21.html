The idea

Buy genuine pre-owned Nike shorts and Ralph Lauren polos in bulk from Pakistani second-hand wholesalers on Fleek (joinfleek.com: shipping included, buyer protection). Have your original designs printed in Pakistan before they ship. Sell them in Sydney as one-offs to 16–21 year olds.
