Two ideas worth knowing

Johnathan Thurston: "too small, too skinny"
RUGBY LEAGUE · ONE OF THE GREATEST EVER
JT grew up in a housing commission house in Brisbane. At 15 he went off the rails, hanging out with what he called the wrong crowd. His parents moved him to a rugby league school in Toowoomba, where he learnt discipline. He later said he didn't want to go, but it was the best move of his life. Then big clubs including the Broncos, Storm and Roosters all knocked him back as too small. He worked in a supermarket butcher section, kept playing, and the Bulldogs finally gave him a shot in 2002.
What this means for you: 15 is just the start of the story. A change of scene, a team and a bit of discipline can turn it into the start.
Sources: Sydney Morning Herald, "Toowoomba's quiet achiever becomes the talk of the town" (2003); ABC Conversations (2018/2020); Brisbane Times (2018); NRL.com player profile.
"The average of five"
BUSINESS SPEAKER JIM ROHN (ATTRIBUTED)
"You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with." The line is usually credited to Jim Rohn, a US entrepreneur and speaker. Researchers can only trace it in print back to a 2005 book that quotes him, so treat it as a popular saying, not a law of science. The idea behind it is simple, though: the people around you shape your habits, your goals and what feels normal.
What this means for you: you get to choose who's in your five. Start with the self-check on the last page.
Source: Quote Investigator, "You Are the Average of the Five People You Spend the Most Time With" (2022).
