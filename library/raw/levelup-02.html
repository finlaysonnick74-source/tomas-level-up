THE GAME PLAN · PHASE BY PHASE

What each phase looks like

PHASE 1ResetWeeks 1–4
Goals
home at Dad's by 4:30pm on school days, then Level up time · swap old time slots for new ones (Pillar 1) · at school on time every day · phone handed in at night · gym with Mum · try a casual session at two clubs · pick your top 2–3 business ideas (the 6-question check, section 7).
Milestones & rewards
Scorecard filled in every week · 2 weeks on time → domain name + a month of an AI coding tool · 4 weeks on time → first stock order or start-up kit.
PHASE 2Build habitsMonths 2–3
Goals
Join one team and train at least twice a week · 4+ ticks on every scorecard row · learn Cursor and GitHub · build version 1 of your site or app · run a small one-weekend test of your idea.
Milestones & rewards
Website or app live · first real customer feedback · 6 weeks of training → sports gear or a business upgrade you choose.
PHASE 3LaunchMonths 3–6
Goals
Open for real customers · keep school and training steady through the summer holidays · maybe do a Bronze Medallion (section 5) · track every dollar in and out.
Milestones & rewards
First sale · first 10 customers · first A$100 profit → Dad matches it with another A$100 into the business.
PHASE 4Level upMonths 6–12
Goals
Reinvest profit · add a second product or service · maybe a part-time job or club role · help a younger kid at your club · 12-month review with Dad.
Milestones & rewards
A full term of 4+ ticks on every row → a day at work with Dad · 12 months → a celebration you plan together.
Seasonal idea? Some businesses (like summer streetwear) need to move faster, with stock ordered in Phase 1. That's fine. The phases are a guide, not a timetable.
