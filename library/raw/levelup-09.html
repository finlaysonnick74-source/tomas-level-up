How to pick (and actually stick with it)

Try two

Do a casual or trial session at two clubs. Pick the one where you liked the people, not just the sport.

Bring someone

Going with a mate, or meeting one coach first, makes the first session way easier.

Give it six weeks

The first few sessions are always the hardest. Six weeks is when it starts to feel like yours.

A local who trained her way to the top

Layne Beachley: a Manly kid
7× SURFING WORLD CHAMPION
Layne was adopted as a baby by a Manly family. She lost her mum at six and found out she was adopted at eight. She has said the hurt drove her to prove herself. She turned pro at 16, won her first world title in 1998, and finished with seven, including six in a row, a record no other surfer has matched. Today she chairs Surfing Australia.
What this means for you: she learnt to surf on the same beaches you know. Where you start doesn't decide where you finish.
Sources: Wikipedia, "Layne Beachley"; ABC Sunday Extra, "The year that made me"; Sydney Morning Herald (2016); laynebeachley.com FAQs.
