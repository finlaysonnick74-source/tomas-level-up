4Show up

Most of winning is turning up: on time, ready, when you said you would. It sounds simple. It's the habit that separates people who talk about it from people who do it.

Kobe Bryant: the 4:15am phone call
NBA LEGEND · 5 CHAMPIONSHIPS
At the 2012 Team USA camp in Las Vegas, Kobe rang a team trainer at about 4:15am to do extra conditioning. When the trainer came back for the 11am scrimmage, Kobe was still there. He'd decided he wanted 800 made shots before practice, and he'd only just finished. Kobe's own explanation: start earlier than everyone else, and by year five or six the gap is so big nobody can catch you.
What this means for you: you don't need a 4am alarm. You just need to do the thing when you said you would, again and again. Small daily reps add up to a gap nobody can close.
Sources: Fox Sports, "The insane 4am training story that defined Kobe's Mamba Mentality" (2020); The SportsRush (2022) quoting Kobe on his training schedule.

The four habits behind every story

1. Show up

Kobe at 4:15am. JT training while working in a supermarket. Being there, on time, beats talent that doesn't turn up.

2. Keep your word

Do what you said, when you said. Customers, coaches and investors all notice, and it's the fastest way to earn trust.

3. Look after the engine

Sleep, training and food. Founders treat their body like the business's most important machine.

4. Choose your circle

JT's new school and team. Layne's surf crew. Your five people shape you more than any book.
Habits are the business plan nobody sees. Get them right and the rest gets easier.
