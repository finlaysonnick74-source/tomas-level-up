Building a real web studio in 21 days

For 21 days, Tomas has chosen to spend 2 hours a day with Dad as his teacher, learning to build websites properly and launching his own business, Tomas Web Studio, for Northern Beaches locals. Every day he builds a real piece of his own site, and on Day 21 (Fri 6 Nov) it goes public: Launch Day.

5How it works
